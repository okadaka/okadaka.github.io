OpenRouter key guide

Get your key. Start free.

Factote is free. OpenRouter is the pay-as-you-go gateway to models, and new accounts get a key without a credit card. Free models work immediately; add credits later for paid models.

Open OpenRouter ↗ Back to Factote home

Factote never charges you. OpenRouter bills only for model usage you choose.

Free models · no credit card

Sign in, copy the key OpenRouter creates, and paste it into Factote. Use free models with daily limits.

Paid models · add credits

Add a small credit balance, create a key with a spending cap, and use open-weight or frontier models.

Easy steps

The two screenshots below are the only OpenRouter-specific steps a new account needs for free models. Paid models are optional.

01 Account

Create your OpenRouter account

Go to openrouter.ai/sign-in and continue with Google. Choose Individual — you can change it later.

02 Copy the key

Copy the key OpenRouter creates for you

On Your workspace is ready, copy the key beside Your API Key. This is the only time the full key is shown. If you lose it, create another key later.

No credits needed for free models. The key starts with a $100 spending cap and an expiry date as a safety limit. You can lower the cap or revoke the key anytime.

03 Optional credits

Add credits for paid models

Free models need no payment. To use paid models, add credits at openrouter.ai/settings/credits. OpenRouter may add a small credit-purchase fee; Factote does not bill you.

With credits, Factote can use over 400 models from providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), xAI (Grok), DeepSeek, Meta, Mistral, NVIDIA, Tencent, Z.ai (GLM), Moonshot AI (Kimi), and Xiaomi.

After credits are added, open a new chat or use Recheck Key Status in the key banner. Factote then switches the default chat and the System Model to GLM 5.3 Flash.

04 Safety rail

Set a key budget (recommended)

At openrouter.ai/settings/keys, create a key with a spending limit — for example, $5. A key limit caps what that key can spend; you can revoke it at any time.

You can also lower the limit on the key OpenRouter created for you. Factote never needs your OpenRouter password.

Usage-based, not a subscription. OpenRouter charges only for the tokens actually used on paid models; there is no monthly plan. Most commercial models cost from a few cents to several dollars per million input or output tokens, so even a small balance goes a long way. Check current prices in OpenRouter's model list, sorted lowest price first. Use the Table view toggle there to compare input and output prices side by side.

05 Connect

Paste the key into Factote

Factote asks you to create a password when you add the key. The key is encrypted with that password in your browser before it is saved to your Google Drive. The password itself is never stored.

If you forget the password, you can reset it and add the key again. Your facts, summaries, and saved chats stay in Drive.

Safety rails

You stay in control of spend and access. Factote is free; OpenRouter is the only party that bills for models.

Key budget

A key-level spending cap limits how much that key can spend. Set it low, raise it when needed, or revoke the key.

Account credits

Paid models draw from your OpenRouter credit balance. When it runs out, Factote detects the error and offers free models.

Free daily limits

Free models are limited to 50 requests/day without purchased credits, or 1,000/day after 10 credits have been purchased.

If something goes wrong

Factote shows a plain-language explanation in the app. These are the common cases.

What you see What it means What to do
Key rejected The key is invalid, revoked, disabled, or expired. Create or copy a valid key and update it in Factote settings.
Key expired The key reached its expiry date. Create a new key in OpenRouter and update Factote.
Out of credits The account has no usable paid balance. Add credits, or switch this chat and the System Model to free.
Key limit reached The key's own spending cap is exhausted. Raise/reset the key limit, or use free models.
Free-key limit The daily free-model allowance is used up, or a free key is throttled. Wait for the UTC reset, add credits, or use a paid model.
Free model busy The free model or its provider is rate-limiting requests. Retry, choose another free model, or switch to a paid model.
Wrong defaults Factote is using free models because no credits were detected. Add credits, then choose the paid conversion in Factote.

How Factote uses the key

Factote talks to OpenRouter directly from your browser. The key is used for model requests, the key-status check, and nothing else.

The key is encrypted with your Factote password before it is saved to Google Drive. Factote's own servers never receive it. Read the privacy note.

Ready with a key? Factote will check it and pick the right defaults.
