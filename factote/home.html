01 / THE IDEA

One memory. Every model.

Factote distills durable facts from your chats and keeps them in your own Google Drive. Choose whichever model fits the moment — your memory comes with you.

Portable memory

Facts about you stay with you, not inside one vendor's silo. Use them with any model, on any device.

Switch without starting over

Move a conversation to another model for a second opinion. The context and your facts follow the chat.

No lock-in

Your Drive, your key, your choice of model. Nothing about your memory is trapped in a vendor silo.

  • Turn Fact Memory off for any chat with Off the Record.

05 / THE PRIVACY

Private by architecture.

Factote is a static app, not a service that sits in the middle of your conversations. The browser talks to OpenRouter directly.

Your browser the app and your key
→
OpenRouter your account, your budget
→
Chosen model DeepSeek, Claude, GPT…
Google Drive · facts, summaries, and settings encrypted key · plaintext saved items

Chats go browser → OpenRouter. Factote has no server that stores them. OpenRouter's privacy terms apply to the request.

Your key is encrypted with your Factote password before it is saved to Drive. The password is never stored. Facts and saved items are plaintext in your Drive, so anyone with Drive access can read them.

Web search and page reading use third parties. Search queries go to the search provider, and page URLs go through the configured fetch relay. Those providers can see the request.
