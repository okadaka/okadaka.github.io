Privacy Policy

Where your data lives, which services receive it, and what Factote does not collect.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Summary

Factote is a browser app for AI chats with user-owned memory. Factote does not operate a server that stores your chats, facts, summaries, or provider keys. The data you choose to save lives in your own Google Drive. Messages go directly from your browser to the AI provider you configure.

Open chats and local data

Open chats stay in this browser tab and are discarded when you close them unless you save a transcript or summary. Factote may keep some settings, theme choices, model preferences, and bounded diagnostic logs in this browser's local storage. Diagnostics are copied only when you explicitly ask for them.

Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, Factote stores the files you save in the Factote folder in your Drive. That includes chat transcripts, summaries, facts, settings, and the encrypted provider key.

Saved items and facts are unencrypted text. Anyone with access to your Google Drive can read them. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to Drive storage.

OpenRouter and AI providers

Chat messages, prompts, and any context you enable are sent directly from your browser to OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to. Factote does not proxy or retain those requests on its own servers. OpenRouter's and the model provider's privacy terms apply.

Provider key

Your OpenRouter key is encrypted with your Factote password before it is saved to Drive. Google stores only the encrypted file. Your password is never stored and cannot be recovered. If you forget it, you can reset the password and add the key again; your saved items and facts remain in Drive.

Web search and page reading

When you use web search, the search query goes to the configured search provider. When Factote reads a page, the URL passes through the configured fetch relay, and the relay operator can see the requested URL and returned page content. The content then comes back to your browser. Factote does not store those requests on its own server.

Usage data and analytics

Factote does not collect usage analytics or sell personal data. There are no advertising trackers in the app. Diagnostic logs stay in your browser unless you choose to copy them for support.

Retention and deletion

  • Close a chat without saving to discard it from the tab.
  • Delete summaries and facts in Factote to remove the corresponding Drive files.
  • Revoke Factote's Google access from your Google Account at any time.
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove local settings and diagnostics.

Google Drive version history or backups may retain copies of deleted content according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Factote changes. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact provided with your Factote deployment.
