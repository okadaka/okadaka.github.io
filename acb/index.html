01 / Why ACB

One cost base. Every account.

The Canada Revenue Agency requires investors to track the cost basis of equities such as stocks and ETFs when calculating capital gains and losses. The average cost must be tracked per ticker across all of your brokerage accounts. Individual brokerages cannot do this for you. They also have limits on how long they store statements, so old data may be hard to surface later. For transactions in a USD account, you also have to use the Bank of Canada daily average exchange rate for conversion to ACB in CAD.

Non-registered

Track every buy and sell.

For taxable accounts, every buy/sell transaction must be tracked indefinitely because each one affects ACB and future capital gains taxes.

Registered

No ACB required.

RRSP, TFSA, RESP and RRIF accounts are not taxed on capital gains and do not impact ACB of securities in non-registered accounts.

02 / Financial tracking

Data You Can Track

Built around the CRA cost-base rules for non-registered accounts, with simpler tools for registered accounts and the events that move holdings between them.

01

Accounts

Track all of your registered and non-registered (taxable) accounts. ACB is automatically calculated across non-registered accounts. For joint accounts the app attributes quantities, ACB costs and gains/losses in a 50/50 ownership split.

02

Transactions

Record transactions with cost basis for CAD and USD accounts. Record exchange rate for USD account transactions.

03

Snapshots

Because ACB is not used for registered accounts, you can use Snapshots feature to track such holdings with a simpler method. Instead of recording every transaction since inception, you can periodically reconcile the quantity and cost against an account statement.

04

Account Transfers

Sometimes you have to move assets between brokerage accounts or between CAD and USD accounts. The app helps you log the moves for future reference and track completion.

05

Stock Splits

Stocks and funds periodically split or consolidate. This makes it harder to track post-split ACB. Enter the split date and ratio, and the app adjusts quantities and ACB automatically.

06

Portfolios

Optionally group accounts into named portfolios for reporting and filtering. Portfolio membership does not change the underlying accounts or ACB.

04 / App and data

Private by design. Portable by default.

Data

Your Data is Yours, in Open Format

All data and reports are stored in standard XLSX files protected by a password. You can open them in Excel, Numbers or Pages.

On save, the files are bundled into a password-protected ZIP archive that you can store anywhere. The archive is designed to remain readable without this app, so your records are not locked in.

Privacy

Security

Your data never goes to a server. The app runs entirely in your browser and writes data only when you click Save. Files are encrypted with your password, and no server ever sees that password.

Because the files are encrypted, you can keep them on Google Drive, iCloud, a USB stick or another backup location with reduced risk of casual access.

Backups

Safety

Every save creates a new time-stamped, password-protected archive. The app never overwrites your existing archive, so you can keep as many versions as you want.

If a change was a mistake, load an earlier archive and continue from there.

Portability

Portable App

The entire app is bundled as a single HTML file with no external runtime dependencies. You can save it locally and open it in a browser.

You can archive the app alongside your data so both remain usable in the future.
