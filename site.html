Description

This 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom furnished townhouse is elegant throughout. Lush greenery can be enjoyed from every window of the 940-square-foot home. The townhouse was built in Craftsman style with attention to detail. Tasteful décor, hardwood flooring, and fir doors and trim throughout ensure that every inch of this beautiful home exudes grace. Large private windows and high ceilings create a light, airy feel.

This townhouse features comfortable modern furnishings. A spacious living room invites you to unwind after a long day. Dimmable warm lighting creates a cozy ambience. The dining area, with a custom-made sequoia table, seats six. The kitchen is equipped with everything you need to cook and features quartz countertops and high-end appliances, including a Miele dishwasher, a new gas stove, and a Fisher & Paykel fridge.

The primary bedroom has ample closet space behind mirrored sliding doors, a comfortable queen bed, and French doors that open onto a balcony. The second bedroom has a queen bed and a desk. The primary ensuite and the second bathroom both have full baths with glass shower enclosures and modern sinks. The unit is stocked with luxury towels and linens. A Miele washer and dryer are available in the unit.

The private garden awaits when you want to dine alfresco, barbecue, or simply enjoy Vancouver’s gorgeous weather.

This townhouse occupies the entire first floor (above ground level) of a boutique development. Living in a residential neighbourhood, you can enjoy Kitsilano’s tranquillity while being less than ten minutes from the beaches and downtown.

Just down the street, you will find a grocery store. Stroll down Granville Street for a wide range of restaurants, shops, bakeries, and amenities. Or hop on a bike and ride the Arbutus Greenway, the dedicated bike path just one block from the townhouse. Buses to UBC and downtown stop just a block away.
